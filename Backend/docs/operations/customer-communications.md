# Customer communication templates

**Owner:** Support Lead, with Product and Legal approval
**Gate:** Friendly Pilot (Runbook §9) · **Criterion:** §15.6

**Status: DRAFT. None of these are approved.** They are written so approval is
the remaining step rather than authorship. §11 governs the rules they follow.

## §11 rules these follow

- Say what is known, what is not yet known, and when the next update comes
- Never speculate on cause before it is established
- Never state customer data was unaffected before that is verified
- One owner per incident writes every customer-facing line
